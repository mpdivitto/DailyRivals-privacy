Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 10, 2026

DailyRivals is built to be simple and private. This policy explains what data the app stores, where it goes, and what we never do with it.

What We Collect

DailyRivals only stores information you enter directly:

  • Your first name (entered at setup)
  • Your daily game scores and results
  • Group name and member names (only if you create or join a sharing group)

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

Where Your Data Lives

  • On your device — all data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Your private iCloud — data syncs across your own devices via your personal iCloud account. Only you can see it.
  • Shared groups (optional) — if you create a sharing group and invite others, only the people you personally invite can see that group's scores. This sharing is powered by Apple's CloudKit.

Third-Party Services

DailyRivals uses Apple iCloud / CloudKit for data sync and optional group sharing. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud data: apple.com/legal/privacy

We use no advertising networks, no analytics services, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Sharing

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party — ever. The only time your data leaves your device is to sync to your own iCloud account, or to a sharing group you explicitly created and invited people to.

Children's Privacy

DailyRivals does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not contain age-restricted content.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by deleting the app from your device. This removes all locally stored data. iCloud data can be deleted through Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → DailyRivals → Delete Data.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
